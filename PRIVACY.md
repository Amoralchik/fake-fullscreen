# Privacy policy — Fake Fullscreen – Theater Mode

**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

Fake Fullscreen does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, crash
  reporting or tracking of any kind.
- **No network requests.** It never contacts a server. All of its code ships
  inside the extension package; nothing is downloaded or run remotely.
- **Settings only.** Your choices (on/off switches, per-site rules, player
  container rules, appearance) are saved with your browser's extension
  storage (`storage.sync`, or `storage.local` as a fallback). If you use
  browser sync, your browser vendor syncs them between your devices under
  its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the extension removes them.
- **Page access.** To work on whatever site you watch video on, the
  extension's scripts run in web pages. They look only at video and player
  elements and the layout around them, to put the video into the in-tab
  theater view. They read no page text, form data, passwords or cookies,
  and they send nothing anywhere.
